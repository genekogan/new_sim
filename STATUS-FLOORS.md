# Floors, steps and seat tops (tranche team/TRANCHE-MARS-FLOORS-20261026.md)

## Diagnosis (verified against the code, 2026-10-26)

**Why Ember stood under the Chiba deck.** The deck is not physics at all. `core/mars-chiba@2` is a mesh def whose footprint
sidecar (`worlds/core-assets/mars/chiba.footprint.json`, sha 81961faa…) was generated by
`tools/god-skill/scripts/mars-two/build.mjs` for a *non-authored* building: it carries 99 content obstacles and levels
`[0, 0.53]`, but **no rooms, no terraces, no surfaces** (`baked.surfaces = []`; the build drops "plywood floor"/"Timber,
floors and stairs" meshes from obstacles and never adds them as floors). Measured from the GLB (probe
`team/scratch/mars-floors-20261026/deck-tris.mjs`): the auditorium/gallery deck is one up-facing plate at **y 0.374**,
def frame x [-1.64, 11.25], z [-5.39, 5.80] (≈ 12.9 × 11.2 m); the furniture on it (presenter box, projector table,
navy sofas) is placed at y 0.373 in the mars-two template. So in a ground world `GroundNav.standY` (spatial/tiled.ts)
finds no floor slab there and returns the terrain (0): the Soul's feet are 0.37 m under the plate. Natives "on the deck"
are only there because they were placed at that height and have not walked (any walk tick recomputes y from standY).
- The engine already has the right rule for level-0 slabs: `GroundNav.standY` raises the feet to a slab ≤ FLOOR_RISE
  (0.5 m) above the ground, and `walk.ts` spreads a step over ticks. What is missing is (a) the plate itself, and (b)
  the server bridge would make a plate at 0.374 its *own level* anyway: `engine-bridge.ts levelFor` treats only floors
  within GROUND_BAND = 0.35 m of the terrain as level 0, so a 0.374 floor would need stairs. (c) The engine's other body
  placements (`engine.ts` stall release ~:940, relocation ~:1112/:1116, set-down land ~:1180, birth ~:1246, and
  `ground-host.heightAt`) use `standHeight(field)` = bare terrain, not `standY` — so even with a plate, a Soul born,
  released or relocated onto it would start under it.
- The coordinator's pointer (`standHeight` + `spatial.base.levels`) is right about the callers, but the walking body's y
  comes from `mars/viewer/engine/locomotion/walk.ts` (`gn.standY`), which already handles slabs.

**Why Ember sat inside the presenter box.** Same root cause. `core/mars-presenter-timber-box@2` has a correct seat slot
(p y 0.41, height 0.41, box 0.5 × 0.41 × 0.46). It stands on the deck at y 0.373, so its top is at 0.78. The engine's sit
puts the sitter's root at `standY` at the slot (`social/actions.ts` transition) = terrain 0, and the seated pose is the
seat height above the root: hips at ~0.41 while the box spans 0.373–0.78 — inside the box. `sit-ground:here` is not the
culprit: its spot needs 0.45 m clearance (`nearestStandableV1`) and the box is a fine-raster obstacle.

**Other Mars buildings with unlisted low floors** (same probe, up-facing area in 0.06–0.6 m): tool-camp (0.38–0.46,
~120 m² + 120 m²), freeside (0.38: 27 m², 0.50: 65 m²), west-racks (0.36–0.42, ~140 m²). Mimos/kitchen/showers are
authored (rooms + stairs in layout.mjs). Not in this tranche's bar; listed for a follow-up.

## Plan (smallest change; natives and Souls share one rule)
- F1a `apps/server/src/god/floor-plates.ts`: measured floor plates of mesh defs whose footprints carry none, by def
  name for every revision (the SEAT_HEIGHTS precedent: worlds keep their stored @2 bytes). Chiba deck first.
- F1b `engine-bridge.ts derive`: a def's plate (and any level-0 floor of a point def) within STEP_UP = 0.45 m of the
  terrain is level 0 (a step, walked onto); higher is its own level (stairs/opening needed). Then `GroundNav.standY`
  stands every body on it (walk ticks), natives and Souls alike.
- F1c `engine.ts`: births / releases / relocations / set-downs use the nav's `standY` (slabs included), via one
  ground-bridge helper `standOn(spatial, x, z)` (swim root kept).
- F2: sit capability computes each seat's top above the feet the sitter will have (`standY` at the slot) — a thing
  standing on a floor the walker does not know (seat top > slot height + 0.15) refuses `seat.inside`; > body max
  `seat.too-high`; both with the nearest usable alternative.
- F3: `here`/`around` name level-0 floors ("Chiba: raised floor 0.37 m, a step up"); receipts' `endedAt` carry
  `floor` and `y`; sit offers say "on top of the presenter timber box (0.41 m)".
- F4: unit tests + local Mars-two Playwright at the Chiba gallery.
