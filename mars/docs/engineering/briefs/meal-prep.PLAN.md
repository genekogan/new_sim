# Bounded communal meal preparation — next candidate

Not implemented or released. Root serial worktree `codex/meal-prep-mvp`, based on
chess evidence084f492. Preserve historical work, current rigs and authoritative
execution; no new helper while historical work occupies the three shared slots.

Visible outcome: prepare a simple cold shared dish from two finite ingredient
containers at an existing table, mix it with a held utensil, then serve/share it
through the existing food break. Ingredients diminish, the same serving vessel
retains preparation progress, and interruption leaves coherent unfinished food.
This proves actual preparation rather than starting with a magically full pot.
It does not claim heat, chopping, fire safety, a documented Mars recipe or kitchen.

Public source establishes the family, not the staging: Mars's Creative AI Bootcamp
2025-09-12 describes participant effort in the kitchen and meals emerging through
that effort (`~/Mars/docs/substack/posts/2025-09-12-creative-ai-bootcamp.md`).
The recipe, people, timings and arrangement here are explicitly hypothetical.

Use ordinary take/put/walk and existing hand-point expression. Preparation reserves
actor, utensil, ingredient containers, serving vessel and relevant support; no pose
or support overwrite, new anatomy solver, replacement object or competing mover.
Contents/preparation state persist on objects. Unprepared/partial food cannot be
silently served as a finished meal; refusal must explain remaining preparation.
Preserve legacy prepared-food behavior and capacity assumptions explicitly.

Acceptance: two feasible arrangements; finite ingredient conservation; same object
identities/support; meaningful visible utensil movement; interruption before and
after a committed ingredient transfer; resumed partial preparation; player handoff;
unavailable ingredient/equipment, moved support and occupied resources; no stranded
claims, deterministic recorded execution and exact quiescent JSON state. Inspect
actual Play and reset lifetime before adding it to the required combined gate.

Stop/escalate if ingredient handling needs new bespoke anatomy, unrestricted cutting,
thermal physics or a general recipe planner. Narrow to the two-container cold-dish
contract without presenting it as cooking. Sleep remains a separate small supported
interaction: existing construction prone poses are specialized and must not be
repurposed as a sleeping activity merely by changing its label.
