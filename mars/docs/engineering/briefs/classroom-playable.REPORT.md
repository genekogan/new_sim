# Playable classroom integration — candidate

Hypothetical /lab maps for four/eight guests: start a lightning-talk session,
queue a question, advance the presenter, request departure, direct an actor to the
courtyard, or end the session during execution. The same registered Runtime owns
movement, seating, display and handoff. Ground floor, fixed furniture, no historical
room assumptions. A bounded head turn communicates shared attention while seated
bodies keep their authoritative seat pose.

Objects and coordinator are selected from stable release13. Old wave2 construction,
exact item fingers, complex vehicle recovery and advanced camera candidates remain
on their original branches; no tests or goldens were removed to make this milestone
independent of those unreleased changes.

Current limits: no joining/rejoining, automatic session resumption, furniture editor,
projector media pipeline, autonomous attendance or dialogue. Arrangements are usable
fixtures, not a general furnishing planner. Visible social expression starts with
floor/attention feedback and bounded head turns; gestures and conversation turn-taking
remain follow-ups. Individual manipulation uses existing actions; held objects refuse
classroom admission instead of being silently dropped.

Validation: coordinator19/19, objects4/4, real maps6/6, parent-claim guard1/1; source scan, typecheck and lint pass. Independent departure and directed intervention demonstrated; all complete classroom cycles assert no failed action records as well as zero violations/resource cleanup.
