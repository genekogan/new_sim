# Bounded computer music-making and listening — next Mars MVP pursuit

2026-10-01, separate root-only worktree based on the unreleased reading candidate.
Not online and no implementation/validation claim. Never dirty the gating reader.

Visible outcome: a performer uses an existing usable computer to make a short
original electronic score while up to two guests arrive, sit and listen. Actual
sound and screen feedback follow authoritative progression. Interruption safely
releases the ordinary physical children and retains the same computer purpose;
explicit resumption continues it. A listener may leave without ending the solo
performance. Missing equipment, unsuitable/occupied seating and foreign player
control have named coherent refusal or cleanup behavior.

This is hypothetical staging, not a historical performer, score or room. The
public Mars2026 recap describes music workshops and live music including an
algorave; Painted Canyons describes live coding. These sources establish the
representative activity family, not the proposed props/people/timing:
- `docs/substack/posts/2026-05-18-recap-of-mars-2026.md` in the Mars archive.
- `docs/substack/posts/2025-06-12-painted-canyons.md` in the Mars archive.

Reuse `work_at`, ordinary seat/walk ownership and the existing computer appearance
and attribution. A logical music coordinator must not acquire a second physical
mover or make unrelated children lose control. The score is a bounded recorded
note sequence, not an LLM-driven musician or new universal performance language.
Audio is optional browser expression explicitly enabled by a user gesture; engine
state/determinism never depends on playback, device clocks or continuous model calls.
Mute/pause/reset/dispose must stop sound and release owned browser resources.

Use current rigs and existing computer/seated poses. Do not add instrument-specific
anatomy, new clips, keyboard finger choreography or acoustic instruments. If a
credible supported interaction repeatedly demands bespoke anatomy, escalate and
bound scope before expanding. Visible score progression, sound, performer purpose
and listener attention matter more than fine contact at this viewing distance.

Acceptance: solo and two-listener arrangements, actual computer approach, score
progress/completion, safe interrupted bookmark and resumption, participant departure,
unavailable equipment, occupied seats, player precedence, deterministic repetition,
no claims/leaks, and actual Play captures with sound lifecycle checks. Focused tests
first; full combined clean gate and inspected production proof before integration.
Stop or narrow if reuse needs a general animation, music or planning subsystem.

File ownership: new `engine/activities/computer-music.ts`, focused tests/scenario,
small registry/catalog additions only if required; Lab scenario/HUD/read-only hook;
small existing computer screen adapter and isolated audio expression. No historical
content, geometry, camera policies or other worktree edits. Root remains serial
while historical specialists use the shared helper capacity.
