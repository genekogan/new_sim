# G006 V2 cue, anchor and bounded provenance candidate

Core-owned `packages/protocol/src/station-v2.ts` adds strict separate
`station-cue/2`, `station-anchor/2`, `station-live/2`, `station-history/2` and
`t:station-v2` codecs. This component installs no journal producer, network
negotiation, timer, audio or default. Existing V1 bytes and parsers are unchanged.

V2 cues add `eraRevision`; their content address includes it. Their actual public
gathering observation retains its originating rules/tick/dt. V1 cues remain exact
variants with origin era 0. Both use the existing finite 128-beat, four-cycle,
half-open interval and two-piece policy. A physical checkpoint never restarts it.

V2 anchors explicitly name current `physicalRulesVersion`/`physicalEraRevision`
and nullable `cueRulesVersion`/`cueEraRevision` alongside the actual tick, shared
wall-clock sample, rate/pause/availability and exact beat. V1 anchors are usable
only in physical era 0. Later eras need an actual recorded V2 anchor. Missing,
stale, paused, unsupported or non-unit-rate data yields no scheduling phase.

Live views contain the immutable original `station`, current `physical` head,
exact cue variant and its independently validated `cuePhysical` originating
head. This is a bounded two-head refinement of the proposal: an old cue from an
earlier era cannot be checked against current rules or a guessed predecessor.
Both heads include exact public physical source when their revision is positive.
They are authoritative publisher projections, not signatures or proof of an
unseen complete journal chain. `anchor:null,fresh:false` starts nothing.

History adds requested `tick`, authoritative exclusive `physicalUntilTick` or
null for the last known era, recorded `sourceCutoff:{seq,tick}`, and nullable
`phase`. The publisher must resolve that actual native era and upper boundary.
Queries before its effective tick, at/after its exclusive end, beyond the
recorded cutoff, or with a later anchor are rejected. A null cue/phase/anchor is
an explicit silent gap; current-head repair or future knowledge is prohibited.
The cutoff is a recorded frontier, not the current unlogged idle clock.

Tests use synthetic station/row metadata around the exact immutable 8d8 native
physical transition. They qualify strict codecs, phase continuity and privacy
refusals only. Canonical paired transaction, snapshot/genesis/crash proofs,
installed V1 silence, V2 App/World consumers and two-listener audio remain open.
Initial 13/14 retained: the rejection assertion expected a later V1-version
diagnostic, while the earlier physical-origin guard correctly refused the old
anchor. Only that expected diagnostic changed; no guard was weakened.
