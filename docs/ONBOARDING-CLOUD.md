Distinguish Gene's decisions, delivered code, dated source evidence and your
proposal. Only Gene moves a ledger item to Decided. The frontier folder records
explicit decisions from a discussion; do not record your own preference as one.

Economy, terrain, permissions, project ontology and self-change remain open or
deferred as indicated by the ledger. Changes to the fork are possible within a
separately authorized task; discussion itself does not authorize implementation,
production writes or edits to protected original checkouts.
