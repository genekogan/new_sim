## Review against `Y5-REWIND-BRIEF.md`
Everything the brief asks for is in the branch, and I found no defect in it alone:
- `POST /v1/god/builds/:id/rewind {to, dry?, force?}` and `build rewind [id] --to k [--dry] [--force]` work as specified.
- It reverts every live edit of the build after step k as one R1 preflight and `commitSequence` chain. That covers lead and
  claim subgroups, with interleaved ranges told apart by group and redo chains followed back to the original edit.
- The build stays open at step k with closure re-armed (`lastBatchAt`, no `closureLapsedAt`, `rewinds` history).
  An owner's rewind never makes a dead lead look alive.
- Later live edits that would lose their effect are refused with `build.depends` (field `to`, a fix, `limit.to`). That
  includes outside undos and resident-made things. The response names what is removed, restored or changed (count + names),
  and `--dry` changes nothing.
- Panel semantics: `undo --group <open build>` rewinds it to the start and keeps it open, rather than closing it as
  abandoned-with-undo. That keeps the undo redoable, and the lead keeps its lease. Finished builds are unchanged. The choice
  and its reasons are written up at `GodMode.undoGroup`.

Missing tests, now added (`648ce29`): a redone edit is rewound through its redo row; after a rewind the build continues
and the next rewind reverts only the newer work; an edit whose step record was trimmed (over 64 records) refuses a partial
rewind but `--to 0` still works; a resident-made thing inside removed work refuses the rewind.

## Merge hazard with Y4/Y4b (fixed on `cloud/y5-on-y4b-20261016`, head `f54c2b2`)
Y5 calls `preflightUndo` directly, so merging it onto Y4/Y4b silently skips their undo checks. A co-builder could rewind
the owner's open build through `undo --group church` or the rewind route. A helper is caught there only by the claim
geometry check, with the wrong code. The integration branch merges Y5 onto Y4b. The only conflicts were in `cli.mjs`
(both sides kept, `eden-god.mjs` regenerated). It also makes `rewind()` refuse helpers, refuse a co-builder on another
account's build, and run `foreignEdit` over its targets, with a test. **Merge that branch rather than Y5 alone whenever
Y4/Y4b ship.**
