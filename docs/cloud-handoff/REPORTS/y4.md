## Review against `Y4-CHILD-TOKENS-BRIEF.md`
Covered by the branch already:
- scoped to one claim: route gate (deny by default), the helper must send exactly its own claim field, and a
  pre-planning target check, with `claimCheck` as the geometric backstop;
- TTL plus ending with the claim (release, done, taken), with the build closing, or when the lead token is gone;
- rate limits pooled into the lead's buckets (`poolOf`); verify-use limited by a per-helper budget (default 3, max 10);
- env, land, strike, prayers, build start/done/abandon/pause/resume, new claims, minting, pairing and the doctor are refused (403 `helper.scope`);
- undo and redo only on the helper's own rows in its own claim group;
- every journal row has hash8 + label (`engine` and `genesis` rows are labelled with no hash), and so does every def origin;
- `build claim … --helper [--ttl min]` writes a 0600 helper file and prints only `EDEN_GOD_HELPER=<file>`, never either token.

Wrong, now fixed (`8efdeac`):
- a helper's `move … in:` and `clone` destinations weren't checked at authorisation. Moving or cloning out of the claim
  was refused only at planning (`build.claim`/`build.claimed`), which misses the brief's "refused at authorisation".
  Both are now checked, including a clone's default destination (its source's parent);
- `relate` was in `HELPER_OPS`, but it writes world-wide relationship layers that no claim may touch. It's now refused
  up front as a lead-only op.

Missing tests, now added: the destination escapes above via act, dryrun and preview; a clone of an outside thing into
the claim is still accepted; and a helper ends (401 `helper.expired`) once its lead token row is gone.
