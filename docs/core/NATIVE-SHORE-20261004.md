## Bounds and safety

Only shore_travel and shore_tread enter this fixture registry. Existing native registry/default factories are unchanged and legacy flat walk is unavailable here. The horizontal Spatial grid supplies bounds/static clearance only; it never supplies root height. The base content address includes bed, water, frozen prescribed policy, scene and full native+fixture rules. The exposed geometry functions are frozen. Foreign base/rules and obstacle overrides are refused before runtime restoration.

The qualified human is an upright radius .28m, height 1.8m capsule with free hands and no load, seat, sleep contact or vehicle. Other families are refused by existing native admission and the controller's anatomy checks; no equivalents are invented. Direct travel checks the whole swept terrain disc at grade <=.35, bounds and actual dynamic occupants/object envelopes, then rechecks each tick. A conservative expanded OBB test can refuse some safe paths. There is no route search or automatic sidestep. An obstruction produces named waiting and movement resumes after it clears; this never moves through an occupied path.

The calm fixture prescribes buoyancy: root follows the bed until depth 1.2m, then remains datum minus 1.2m. The path splits at that boundary, with continuous roots and speed <=.9m/s; heading turns before translation. Swim is bounded upright-capsule travel. It does not qualify horizontal stroke/body-rig geometry, hydrodynamics, mass/displacement, currents, waves, fatigue or drowning. Stationary deep-water state is tread, including after cancellation and task/reservation release. Small-load swim, dock supports, boat controller, passenger frames and gangway safety remain future work.
