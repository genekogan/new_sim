# Native ground infrastructure for eight camp residents

This explicit reader upgrades the existing native camp without replacing its
genesis, people, old places, materials, cards or owner history. It is a first
installed physical stage of the approved Mars program. Upper floors, structural
stairs, construction bills/recovery and artificial water remain unfinished.
No default factory, main bootstrap, production service or World asset is changed.

`marsCampExpansionFactory()` retains the original `marsFactory` genesis and rules
until a trusted internal `WorldHost.adoptCampExpansion()` commits a validated
`camp-ground-expansion/1` rules event and world-header revision in one transaction.
The reader also supports the original proving-ground genesis followed by its
existing camp-adoption event. No REST/MCP permission or alternative steering
mechanism is introduced. Preview is detached; accepted births still use the
ordinary lifecycle, native placement and current owner checks.

These are authored finite simulation resources, not economic pricing or material

The new sleeping areas are supported **ground work areas**. They do not certify
the frozen World proposal's narrow 0.9×2m mats, 4cm mattress tops, proposed bunk

dimensions/roots. A visual surface must not translate the roots or manufacture

The upgrade copies the exact native serialized graph and appends only declared
entities/regions and the new physical base. Action numbering, aliases and records
remain. It refuses active adapter programs/departures, nonquiet or dropped-work
checkpoints, foreign bases, obstacle overrides, entity/region/kernel collisions,
stale/tampered transitions and moved headers. Repeating the accepted upgrade
returns its original event and does not create another supply edit. Snapshot
restore refuses other physical bindings and validates the exact policy/base/hash.
Existing station history explicitly refuses this upgrade until a corresponding
atomic station-era carry is qualified; it is not silently dropped or reinterpreted.

The recorder seals the old scene/take at the physical boundary and publishes a
new capture address. Old take IDs/captures remain unchanged; no chunk straddles
the two bases. Ordinary renewal treats camp adoption as a no-op on this already
registered expanded base. Quiet utensil return knows the new home slots. The
existing explicit logged practical restock operation can replenish both service

Camp cards select eight resource bindings only on the new base. Existing four
bindings and minted recipe hashes remain. New residents receive distinct logged
meal/return/sleep recipes. Physical free hands, whole sleep envelope, routes,
food quantities and ordinary reservations still decide whether they can act.
Presence alone never certifies capacity or permits another person's body.
