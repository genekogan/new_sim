The former private projection serialized every retained owner intention into the
mid-mind call. In an actual fifty-revision owner history, that section was 167792
UTF-8 JSON bytes before the new projection and 13422 afterward; all fifty accepted
revisions, words, strictness and evidence stayed in canonical history unchanged.
This measures one own-purpose section, not total prompt tokens or provider cost.

The prospective intentPurposeRecallV1 policy requires source-backed evidence.
It caps selected purpose entries at 12 and their JSON at 16384 UTF-8 bytes. All
strict and current/acting commitments must fit verbatim and with their accepted
qualifiers. Otherwise recall reports mandatory overflow, serializes no incomplete
set as permission to choose, and the model request builder refuses it. Brain
reports the capacity condition privately once per owner-will revision and makes
no new model/reflex choice. Existing native activity and the world clock continue;
no pause, pose cancellation or history deletion is introduced. Explicit owner
withdrawal can clear capacity; it is not automatic forgetting.

Remaining context is ranked by permitted current local names/situation, evidenced
unfinished contribution, importance and revision recency. This selects context,
not a work sequence. Older locally relevant unfinished context may precede newer
unrelated text. Omitted count and at most 12 source references are explicit, with
an indication that more exist; the model cannot invent their words/progress.
All inputs belong to one private world/Soul scope. No global acquaintance mind,
new observation permission, routine object, timetable or permanent index exists.
Legacy defaults remain unchanged; paired /2 records the prospective policy and
retained-only startup refuses missing policy without an append.
