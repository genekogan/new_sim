# Native stopped-dock boat fixture

This optional native Runtime executes two actual people boarding through a
supported ramp/deck/gangway, occupying separate seats, travelling together,
returning to the stopped dock and leaving through the same connector. The
existing living factories and default registry are unchanged. This is the
next W-002 physical producer, following accepted shore `72b005d`.

## Physical and authority boundaries

`mars/viewer/engine/vehicles/boat.ts` publishes frozen `BOAT_LAYOUT` and
`BOAT_POLICY`. The fixed yaw-zero native hull is base-centred at its keel:
`[-4,-.3,7]`. World's proposal frame `[-4,.1,7]` is exactly `.4m` above that
native origin. The native deck/seat root local Y `.8` equals proposed local
Y `.4`; both yield world Y `.5`. Dock native base `[-3,.5,5]` has top `.7`.
This is an explicit coordinate conversion, not a changed hull or water datum.

Ramp connects bank `[4,.52,5]` to `[0,.7,5]`, width `1.5m`; deck entry
`[-4,.7,5.5]` connects to hull `[-4,.5,6.75]` through a `.8m` gangway.
Exactly two seat roots are admitted. Passenger occupancy is reciprocal native
state; a seat never grants pilot or owner permission. The anonymous fixture
explicitly grants its hypothetical physical `pilot` capability separately.
These fixture people are not owner-world Souls.

Board/leave reserve actor, boat, slot and connector. Roots move at most `.35m/s`
and turn at most `4rad/s`; a moving or occupied connector prevents departure.
Cancel drains the supported committed boarding/exit path to a seat or shore
before releasing reservations. A newly blocked path stays supported and retains
its reservation; removing its blocker permits continuation. This is one fixed
admitted corridor, not generalized navigation over arbitrary visual meshes.

Travel translates the whole native boat at `.6m/s`; all actual occupied seat
roots follow atomically. Both admission and each tick check the full swept hull
rectangle against the exact water bed for `.3m` draft plus `.1m` keel margin.
Current human/object envelopes include the conservative upright passenger
height. Current seat frames, bodies, free hands and pilot capability are checked
before movement. Unknown geometry refuses. Mid-route cancel stops the boat with
its passengers seated; exiting requires the exact qualified stopped dock.

Buoyancy is prescribed calm-water draft. Catalog mass zero is hypothetical
fixture metadata, not a displacement calculation. Waves, currents, yaw changes,
hydrostatic stability, heavy loads, fine legs/contact rigs, duck equivalents and
other docks remain unqualified. Body envelopes are conservative upright human
capsules. Missing externally deleted passengers remain visible diagnostics and
block movement; no automatic replacement or authoritative repair is fabricated.

## Public producer and compatibility

Strict code schemas in `packages/engine-contract/src/boat.ts` validate optional
`boat`, `boatPassenger` and `boatTransit` take fields and the published native
layout. The existing take format
and pure reader carry them; absent legacy fields stay absent. Phase, connector,
stopped dock, finite frames and unique occupancy are checked. World should read
the canonical codecs and recorded roots, not create another movement controller.

`nativeBoatFixture()` binds the exact bed, layout, policy, scene and native rules
to its new `native-boat-fixture/1` base. Shore/boat foreign snapshots are refused
in both directions. This does not transition an existing world or reinterpret
history. The isolated fixture uses current 3D envelope clearance instead of the
legacy flat raster clearance; all physical support and obstruction diagnostics
are still checked. Legacy/default runtime invariant selection is unchanged.
