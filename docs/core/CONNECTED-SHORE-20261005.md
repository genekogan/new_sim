## One physical world and one movement authority

The unmodified commons floor, pavilion, deck, tables and benches remain at their
existing coordinates. The reviewed shore bed is translated by (-46, -2, 0):
its dry top meets the commons west boundary at (-30, 0). Water datum becomes -2
and depth is preserved. Two new flat one-metre columns extend the top to x=-28,
so the full human foot disc at the shared gate (-29, 0) is supported by both
native domains. The added shoulder intentionally changes the top-edge normal.
The derived physical base, source content hash, transform, policies and native
rules receive their own address; old shore and commons bases refuse it.

`commons_shore_travel` is one retained native action. Its physical children are
existing `walk_to` and `shore_travel` controllers; every move belongs to the same
Runtime and tick. Commons-to-shore travel walks to the gate, then follows the
actual triangle bed into wading/swimming. Return travel follows the same bed to
the gate and then walks across commons. A native child is admitted at its real
phase; future shore geometry/obstructions and the actual first child are checked
before initial admission. No projected body, relocation, authored pose or fake
terminal is used to claim execution.

The parent owns the actor claim throughout the handoff. It snapshots its actual
child/index and preserves deferred cancellation until the child ends. Deep-water
cancellation leaves the real body buoyant and treading; it releases claims and
permits a new return direction without a snap to either floor. The shore segment
is still a bounded direct route, not general terrain pathfinding or swimming
anatomy/forces. Unsafe or occupied routes refuse or wait.

Flat-grid walking cannot enter the slope/water domain. Other ordinary native
commons actions check their actual actor claims after their pure plan and refuse
an unqualified wet/slope actor before admission. Only a supported dry gate permits
the native walk handoff; a face action in deep water cannot reset the swimmer to
idle. Native shore actions own water support, while existing commons invariants
continue to apply on commons. Static-floor clearance is delegated only for an
actual certified shore contact/root; full shore support and speed invariants
remain active. Human separation and native ownership/load checks remain active.

Public geometry readouts are detached; mutating them cannot change the internal
physical bed or capture. No new root dependency, default native registry entry,
server setting, account, API or W/G wire shape is introduced. Existing strict
`native-shore-scene/1` and addressed native take capture carry the combined data.
