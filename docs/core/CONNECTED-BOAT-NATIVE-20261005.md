## Remaining interfaces and limits

World needs an explicitly addressed combined boat consumer for this exact
physical base, translated dock/boat layout and paired capture. Existing isolated
boat appearance and connected shore acceptance do not establish that consumer.
Core next needs an owned EngineFactory, ordinary intention/activity exposure,
source-backed pilot policy, safe passenger retirement and persistent recovery.
App owns the human permission/consumer counterpart; no implicit grant is proposed.

Two upright seats, straight calm routes, fixed yaw, one exact stopped dock and
free hands are deliberately bounded. General navigation, hydrodynamics,
anatomy families, social composition and a living two-owner boat episode remain
unqualified. No old-world transition, default installation, UI/device/cohort,
realAuth or release claim. Main/push/deploy stay with PM after Gene's handover;
October6 cutoff and fake local minds/$0 remain in force.
